How to play

Two of a kind

  1. Aim along the top of the bowl and drop the waiting cat.
  2. When two cats of the same size touch, they become the next cat.
  3. Chains within a short beat score more. Rokumon, the last cat, does not merge.
  4. The run ends if cats settle above the limit line.

Free play gives three undos. The daily challenge shares one drop order until UTC midnight and allows one undo. Aim with a mouse, a finger, or the arrow keys, then drop.
